
# Hamradio / Amateur radio notes

Doing the license in Norway in autumn of 2026, in the [Oslo group (LA4O)](https://la4o.no/) of [Norwegian Radio Relay League (NRRL)](https://nrrl.no/).
Here are some associated notes on learning about radio.

## Useful references

- [Q-codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_code#Q-codes_as_adapted_for_use_in_amateur_radio).
Standardized shorthand for common question/answers.
- [WebSDR](http://websdr.org/).
Collection of online accessible SDR receivers across the entire world.
- [WSJT-X](https://wsjtx.github.io/wsjtx/index.html). Most popular software for digital communication such as FT8.
- [](https://www.pskreporter.info/pskmap.html). 20m is most popular, then 40/15/17/30.

## Terminology

- QRP operation. Transmitting at low power while maximizing range. Typically up to 5 watts.
- DX / DXing. Making contacts over long distances. 
- Contact. QSO.
- QSL card. Written confirmation of communication between two stations.
